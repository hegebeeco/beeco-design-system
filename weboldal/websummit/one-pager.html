Don't just claim it. Do it, measure it, show it.

beeco is an award-winning Hungarian green lifestyle app and community. We turn employees and residents into a swarm of real, measurable green action, and hand you the numbers to prove it.

Why now

Green claims need proof

  • EU 2024/825: since 27 Sept 2026, unproven generic green claims and offset-based claims are banned.
  • CSRD after Omnibus I: mandatory above 1,000 employees and EUR 450M turnover; most others report with the simpler VSME standard.
  • Companies need concrete, verified action. That is what beeco delivers.
