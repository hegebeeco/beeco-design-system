## 1. Hero
- Szalag: **Free green lifestyle app from Hungary**
- H1: **The green lifestyle app that shows you your next step**
- Lead: Small daily steps towards a green 2050: calculate your footprint, find green places and events nearby, and collect nectar for every step.
- Gombok: **Get the app** (App Store / Google Play – a meglévő DOWNLOAD_BLOCK) · **Talk to us** (→ 5. szekció)
- Apró: The app is currently available in Hungarian.

## 2. Why beeco
- H2: **Climate change feels too big. Small steps don't.**
- Szöveg: Many of us feel that what we can do alone is not enough. But a green 2050 is built from small daily steps: we break them down for you and stay with you all the way.
- Három kártya:
  - **Understand** – Calculate your carbon, water and waste footprint and see where you can improve.
  - **Act** – We show you the next step and where to take it nearby: green places, events, coupons.
  - **Stay motivated** – Every small step earns nectar, inside a community where you are not alone.

## 3. Impact
- H2: **The power of the swarm**
- Számok (a főoldal „Közösség ereje” blokkja):
  - 25,000+ downloads
  - 23,000+ green spots on the map
  - 15 partner municipalities
  - 100+ green events every month
  - 12 professional partners
  - 7,000 trees watered in summer 2025 · 400,000 litres of water · 1,500 volunteer waterers
- Forrás-sor: Tree-watering data: Telex, 21 September 2025.
- 2026-os számok a záró közleményből (2026. október): 1 million+ litres, 2,200+ volunteers, 7,500+ young trees, 15 municipalities.

## 4. Our story
- H2: **Who is behind beeco?**
- Szöveg: An award-winning Hungarian startup built by volunteers and professionals, with university and civic partners. Eight specialist teams, one goal.
- Idővonal:
  - **2023** – beeco is born. Climate Launchpad international final, MVM Edison 3rd place.
  - **2024** – The app and Hungary's largest green map launch. Web Summit, Lisbon.
  - **2025** – ONE Digital Award, 1st place. Hungary's first community tree-watering campaign.
  - **2026** – 15 partner municipalities, the CsicsergŐsz bird campaign, back at Web Summit.
  - **2027** – beeco abroad: more towns, communities and eco-heroes.
